# HR Leave Policy

Department: HR
Owner: HR Operations
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Confidentiality: Internal

## Scope
This policy applies to all full-time employees. Contractors follow the terms of
their individual agreements.

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year, accrued monthly at 2 days per month.

## Carry Forward
A maximum of 10 unused annual leave days may be carried forward into the next
calendar year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 December unless a written
exception is approved by the reporting manager and HR Operations.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is
required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary
caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks. Eligibility begins after 80 days of
continuous employment.

## Applying for Leave
Leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance
for planned leave. Manager approval is required before the leave is confirmed.

## Effective Date and Interpretation
This policy is effective 1 April 2026. For any interpretation question, employees
should contact HR Operations rather than relying on informal guidance.
